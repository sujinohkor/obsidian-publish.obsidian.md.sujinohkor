This site contains my notes on the papers I’ve read. Each set of notes follows one of two formats:
- **RQ / Research Goal**
- **Approach**
- **Findings**
- **Limitations**

or:
- **Problem.** What does this method address, and why was it developed?
- **Core idea.** What is the key idea behind the proposed method?
- **Advantages.** Why is this method better than existing approaches?
- **Novelty.** If this approach is so promising, why hasn’t anyone done it before?
